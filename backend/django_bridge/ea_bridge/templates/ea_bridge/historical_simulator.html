Replay behavior

The chart opens with approximately 30 visible historical candles. Click Play to reveal more candles automatically, Pause to stop, or Step Forward to reveal one completed candle. Drag the timeline to seek. Drag the chart to pan and use the mouse wheel to zoom. Future candles remain hidden from the chart until replay advances. The performance cards show broker trades previously recorded in Ramon's learning journal, restricted to the loaded chart time range and replay cursor. They are not strategy backtest performance. The separate Strategy Backtest form runs isolated estimated trades over a bounded period. The chart loads further candles automatically in pages as replay advances.
